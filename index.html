          Portfolio strategies

          The same plan run six ways, using your numbers. "Savings run out" is at your planned spending. "Carries" is the peak family spending per month, in today's dollars, the strategy supports without ever running short, and "left per child" is the estate if you spend at that level.

          Building the portfolio without a job

          1. Start buying rentals now, one a year, while spending is low. Waiting costs years of rent and growth.
          2. Buy for cash flow, not appreciation. Aim for yearly rent near 10% of the price, such as a $275k duplex or house renting for about $2,300 a month.
          3. Keep running costs near 35% of rent by managing the houses yourself or negotiating management fees.
          4. Keep six months of spending in cash and never buy past that reserve.
          5. Set the family budget to what the portfolio carries. Each extra $1,000 a month needs about $300,000 more invested behind it.

          Projections use smooth average returns, not advice. Have a fee-only planner or CPA check taxes and financing before buying.
